Eye Tracking Cursor

This app uses your webcam to track your eye movements and control a cursor on screen.

  • A working webcam is required
  • Good, even lighting on your face works best
  • Head movement is compensated, but staying relatively still gives best results
  • You'll complete a short calibration before tracking begins
